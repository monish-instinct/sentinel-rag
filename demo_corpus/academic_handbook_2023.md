# Northbridge Institute of Technology: Academic Handbook 2023

SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Northbridge Institute of Technology is fictional; this file exists only to test Sentinel-RAG.

## Attendance

Students must attend at least 75 percent of the scheduled classes in each course to be eligible to sit the end-semester examination. Attendance is calculated separately for each course and is published on the student portal every two weeks.

## Assessment and Late Submission

Assignments submitted after the deadline lose 10 percent of the marks for each day of delay. Submissions more than three days late receive zero marks. Extensions are granted only by the course instructor and must be requested before the deadline.

## Re-examination

A student who fails a course may register for the re-examination by paying a fee of INR 1,500 per course. Registration closes 14 days after the results are published.

## Grading

Grades are awarded on a ten-point scale. A grade point of 4 is the minimum pass grade in every course.

## Library

The central library is open from 8 am to 10 pm on weekdays and from 9 am to 5 pm on Saturdays. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays.
