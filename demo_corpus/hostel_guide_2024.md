# Hostel Residents' Guide 2024

## Timings

Hostel residents must return to the hostel by 10:30 pm on all days. A late return without prior permission from the warden is recorded, and three such records lead to a disciplinary hearing.

## Fees

The annual hostel fee is INR 85,000, which includes accommodation and meals.

## Visitors

Guests may visit the common lounge between 4 pm and 7 pm. Guests are not permitted inside residents' rooms.
