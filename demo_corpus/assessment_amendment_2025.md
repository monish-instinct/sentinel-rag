## Revised Late Submission Rule

With effect from the 2025-26 academic year, the late submission penalty is reduced to 5 percent of the marks for each day of delay. Submissions more than five days late receive zero marks. This amendment supersedes the late submission rule in the 2023 Academic Handbook.
