## Condonation of Attendance Shortfall

The Dean of Academics may condone an attendance shortfall of up to 10 percent for students who were absent on documented medical grounds. A medical certificate must be submitted to the department office within 7 days of returning to classes.

Students representing the institute in national or international sports events are granted on-duty attendance for the days of the event. No condonation is available for absences without documentation.
